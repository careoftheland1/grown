Not only are all of the grocery stores beyond walking distance from where you’re standing right now, but our neighborhood is even further from anywhere food is actually grown. The miles of road between a plate and the ground it came from quietly spend the nutrition out of our food before it ever arrives. And the distance between us and our grocer reduces how often we get fresh food. The fix isn’t a bigger truck. It’s a shorter one: greens grown a few blocks from where they’re eaten, picked when you pick them up. Nutrition nearby.
