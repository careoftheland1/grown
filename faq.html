The Greens

How are they grown?

100% hydroponically—no soil, no pesticides, a fraction of the water used by field-grown greens.

How fresh are they?

Picked the morning of pickup.

How should I store them?

Keep them cool and dry, and eat them within a few days for the best flavor.
